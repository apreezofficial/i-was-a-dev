# 🚀 iwasaDev

Once upon a time, many ex-developers and engineers felt shy to identify with their past—to say, *"I used to code."*

**iwasaDev** exists to change that.

It's a place where former developers and engineers can proudly express who they were, share their journey, reconnect with the community, and wear their experience without hesitation.
